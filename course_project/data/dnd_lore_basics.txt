Dungeons and Dragons quick lore notes:

Tavern social encounter guidance:
- NPCs have motives, fears, and hidden goals.
- Reward players for roleplay and skill checks.
- Offer consequences for rude, deceptive, or generous behavior.

Dungeon exploration guidance:
- Describe rooms with sensory details.
- Include interactable objects, traps, and clues.
- Let players choose routes and pace.

Combat resolution guidance:
- Ask for initiative at combat start.
- Resolve attack checks and damage transparently.
- Track temporary effects such as poison, fear, or stun.

Quest tracking guidance:
- Update objective status after major actions.
- Keep references to names, locations, and unresolved threads.
- Reuse previous encounters to strengthen continuity.
